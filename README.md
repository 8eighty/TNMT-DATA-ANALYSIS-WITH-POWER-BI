**Project Overview**

The TNMT Revenue Dashboard is an interactive Business Intelligence solution developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze and visualize business revenue, profitability, product performance, sales team performance, and geographical sales distribution.The dashboard transforms business data into meaningful visual insights, enabling stakeholders to monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), identify top-performing products and sales teams, and understand revenue contributions across different states.
Designed with a clean, intuitive, and interactive interface, this project demonstrates how data visualization can support strategic decision-making and improve business performance monitoring.

**Project Objectives**

The primary objectives of this project were to:

- Develop an interactive Power BI dashboard for business performance monitoring.
- Track total revenue and profit using key performance indicators.
- Analyze monthly revenue trends and identify performance fluctuations.
- Identify the highest-revenue-generating products.
- Evaluate sales team performance and compare individual contributions.
- Analyze revenue distribution across different states.
- Provide an interactive product category filter for dynamic analysis.
- Present complex business information in an accessible and visually compelling format.

**Dashboard Visualizations**

1. Total Revenue by Month

Tracks revenue performance from January to May.
Highlights monthly revenue fluctuations.
Helps identify periods of stronger and weaker revenue performance.

2. Top Ten Products by Revenue

Ranks the ten highest-revenue-generating products.
Highlights products making significant contributions to total revenue.
Supports product performance evaluation.

3. Top Three Performing Sales Teams

Compares the revenue generated by the three leading sales representatives.
Highlights individual sales performance.
Helps identify high-performing members of the sales team.

4. State Revenue Analysis

Visualizes revenue contributions across different states.
Identifies geographical markets generating the highest revenue.
Supports regional performance evaluation.

5. Product Category Filter

Allows users to filter the dashboard by product category.
Makes it easier to explore specific product segments.
Enables more focused business analysis.
